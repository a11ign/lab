The gates and the one-box harnesses submit their captures to the fleet job service instead of dispatching to a worker themselves (a11ign A11-263, step 4): `gate:stability` (one job, a group per canary, so a canary's repeats stay on one box), `gate:probe-order` (a group of three per page: A1, B, A2; a page whose captures failed is submitted again once, then INCONCLUSIVE), `capture:check` in worker mode (six cases, an unconfirmed identity is a `fleet job recapture`, up to four attempts), `training:repeat` (one group; a transient fault is captured again on the same box, up to three attempts), `eval:capture`, `identity:rate`, `verdict:stability` and `bench:capture` (one group each, in the order the measurement needs). Each takes `--label=<ticket key>` (or `A11Y_FLEET_LABEL`), `--boxes=` or `--box=<name>` (or `A11Y_FLEET_BOXES`), `--wait=` (or `A11Y_FLEET_WAIT`) and `--collect[=<job ID>]`, and with no wait prints the job ID and exits 75; a box is named, never addressed, and an address is ignored when `A11Y_FLEET_BOXES` is set and refused otherwise. The worker pool (`worker-pool.mjs`, `gateWorkers`, `acrossFleet`) and the by-hand wake (`wake-by-hand.mjs`) are removed with their last callers. `training:repeat` prints `sockets recovered` and `polls survived` as `not reported`: the service owns the transport and its records carry neither count.
