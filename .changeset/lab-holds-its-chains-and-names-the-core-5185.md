The lab holds the release and promotion chains, and four of its files no longer assume the core stands three directories above them (a11ign/a11ign#5185, a11ign#5175 follow-up). `package.json` gains `release:gate`, `candidate:gate` and `promote:gated`, the lab-held stages in the order the core ran them before a11ign#5087; each ends in `gate:core-held`, which exits 1 naming `scorer:migration`, `scorer:retired-heads` and `gate:isolation` rather than claiming they passed (whether the publish path runs them at all is a11ign/a11ign#5179). `check-shipped-provenance.ts`, `check-rehearsal-currency.ts` and `promote-model.ts` take the core's root from `coreRoot()` in `src/dataset-paths.ts`: their own variable, else `A11Y_CORE_ROOT`, else the core above a lab laid at `<core>/packages/lab`, otherwise a refusal naming both variables. From a standalone checkout the old default read `/home/<user>/.changeset` and reported on a tree that is not the core's. `promote-model.ts` resolves its root lazily, so importing its pure functions never refuses, and its candidate default is the lab's own `runs/` rather than the core's, so the model `candidate:gate` gates is the one promoted. `src/packaging/package-scripts-resolve.test.ts` refuses a `run` link in the manifest or a workflow that names a script the manifest lacks.
